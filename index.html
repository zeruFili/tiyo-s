meet Tiyo

Welcome to our atelier, where ancestral textile craftsmanship converges with contemporary silhouette.
Tiyo is an independent fashion house dedicated to reimagining authentic handwoven heritage textiles for modern lifestyles. From structured waistcoats and flowing robes to geometric woven kimonos, every creation is meticulously tailored.
Discover the living harmony of ancestral craftsmanship, sustainable pure cotton, and cosmopolitan elegance.
Step into a world where timeless traditions become tomorrow's fashion statements.

The Collection

A vibrant dialogue between ancestral loom artistry and effortless modern leisure. Tiyo transforms authentic hand-loomed cotton and rich geometric weaves into breathable summer blazers, unstructured cardigans, and modern daytime staples.
Tailored for tranquil gardens and sunlit city promenades alike, each garment celebrates cultural permanence with contemporary ease.

Atelier Journal

Behind every Tiyo garment lies a living legacy — master weavers spinning pure cotton yarns, hand-setting wooden looms, and weaving intricate geometric diamond motifs into modern streetwear forms.
We celebrate the intersection of heritage craftsmanship, timeless automotive elegance, and modern urban identity. Garments crafted not for a single season, but for generations.
